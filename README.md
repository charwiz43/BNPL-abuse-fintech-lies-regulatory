## **Overview & Case History**
This repository serves as the official public regulatory record, legal correspondence archive, and operational breakdown surrounding **CFPB Complaint #260717-35668593** involving Affirm, Inc. (NYSE: AFRM) and outside counsel Morgan Lewis & Bockius LLP.
This archive documents the complete timeline, forensic ledger evidence, merchant delivery failures, and regulatory filings arising from automated point-of-sale loan disputes under the Truth in Lending Act (12 C.F.R. § 1026.13, Regulation Z), the Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. § 1681), and Dodd-Frank UDAAP provisions (12 U.S.C. § 5531).
